# Mother Teresa under-read: diagnosis and proposed fix (2026-09-25)

Branch `teresa-underread`. The fix is not merged or deployed, and `src/figures.js` is unchanged.

## Diagnosis

Stored file: **569**, warmth 56, competence 58, ADMIRED (likability unrated), which is below the roster median of 658.

| care | alignment | utility | adaptability | legacy | network | physical | threat | redundancy |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 55 | **42** | 60 | **45** | 65 | 65 | 50 | 22 | 40 |

What drives it, from the prompts on main (`netlify/lib/systemPrompt.js`, `publicRecord.js`):

1. **The care definition only looks at the subject's own circle.** Care is "do right by the people in their life (family, friends, partners, coworkers, community)". A life of service to *strangers* (the destitute and dying) doesn't fit that definition, so her care came out at 55, which is the level of an ordinary person with no evidence. The same blind spot applies to Tubman and Schindler-type records. Tubman scores well regardless because her other dimensions are strong.
2. **Contested opinions are counted as low alignment.** When main's prompt re-scores her, the verdict says so outright: *"her opposition to contraception and abortion count against alignment and care."* That penalises a contested religious and moral view, not conduct toward others. Alignment 42 is the single largest drag on her warmth, because alignment is 30% of warmth.
3. **The fact-check did not strip her care evidence.** The stored verdict has no fact-check record: it was set during the roster rescore, and the fact-check that removes claims ran only on the 40 verdicts it changed. The critical claims about her homes (pain relief, conditions) are stated as documented. Our rules allow that because they are widely reported. They are not being treated as allegations.

## Proposed wording change (minimal; the harm gate and weights are untouched)

`systemPrompt.js`, the care bullet:
- The definition gains *"and by the people they have chosen to serve, including strangers"*.
- Added after the anchors: *"Sustained, costly service to strangers (the poor, the sick, the enslaved, the persecuted) is care, and a lifetime of it can reach 85-95. Documented harm done within that service is weighed as documented; criticism that remains disputed is weighed as disputed and never erases the service."*

`systemPrompt.js`, the alignment bullet, added:
- *"Judge conduct toward others, not contested political, religious or moral opinions: holding a view many people dispute is not low alignment unless it was acted on in ways that harmed people."*

`publicRecord.js`, the care sentence:
- Now covers *"the people they chose to serve, including strangers"*.
- A life of costly service is *"care of the highest order"*, with the same rule for documented versus disputed.

## Side effects and cautions

1. **Possible over-softening.** The branch verdict calls the criticism of her homes "disputed, not settled". The observations (inadequate pain relief, poor hygiene, reused needles) are widely reported by named sources (e.g. *The Lancet*, 1994). What is disputed is the interpretation. The prompt says documented harm is weighed as documented. The model read the whole matter as disputed. If Scott wants those observations to keep weighing, tighten the wording to "documented observations weigh as documented even when their interpretation is disputed".
2. **The roster is stale against the current prompt.** Fresh scoring under the *live* main prompt already moves Thiel 494→609, Musk 540→617 and Einstein 728→744, mostly on utility, legacy and threat. The stored figures predate the anchor recalibration and were hand-edited afterwards. The weekly calibration loop recomputes formulas from stored breakdowns and never re-scores, so it can't see this drift. Updating Teresa alone would compare her fresh score against stale neighbours.
3. **Fixed on the branch: `scripts/rescore-figures.mjs` would have erased fields.** Its line rewrite wrote only name, score, tier, breakdown and verdict, which would drop warmth, competence, quadrant, born and died for every rescored figure. It now keeps all other fields and recomputes the cube from the new breakdown. It still has no fact-check pass. Referrals get one, the roster rescore doesn't.
4. Cost of this test: 14 Sonnet scoring calls plus 9 Haiku fact-checks.

## Recommendation

Merge the prompt fix, then **re-score all 62 figures in one pass under the new prompt**, with a fact-check added to `rescore-figures.mjs`. That puts everyone on one prompt version, so nobody is advantaged by being rescored first. Expect Mother Teresa around 690-700 and broad upward drift for others. Review the full before/after table before pushing. Optionally tighten the documented-vs-disputed wording (caution 1) first.

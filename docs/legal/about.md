# ABOUT THE DEPARTMENT

> The Department of Human Assessment exists to tell you what you are worth. It is wrong about this. That is the point.

## This is satire

The Human Value Index is satire and commentary about social scoring: the idea that a machine should rank people by their "value." It imitates a scoring authority so you can see how absurd, arbitrary and uncomfortable that is. It is not a real ranking, rating agency, background check or government service.

## Scores are opinion, not fact

Every score, tier and verdict on this site is the machine's opinion, written by an AI model (Anthropic's Claude) in the voice of a fictional Overlord. The numbers come from a made-up formula. Nothing here is a statement of fact about anyone, and none of it should be relied on for any decision about a real person.

## Public figures

Public figures are assessed only on their public record: what is published about them, starting with their Wikipedia and Wikidata entries. The Department does not use private information about anyone. Where a verdict mentions harm, it is meant to reflect what the public record documents, such as a conviction, a court finding, or the person's own public statements. If a file gets that wrong, [dispute it](#dispute).

- **Living people never speak in the world.** The figures in the city are sprites. They do not talk, and no words are put in a living person's mouth.
- **The city is a simulation.** Where a figure works, where they go, who they sit with, befriend or fall out with, and every "gossip" line about it, is generated at random by the simulation. None of it describes real events or real relationships, except links the city marks as "known to each other before intake," which come from the public record.
- **The founders of the world's faiths are not assessed.** Those files are sealed by policy.
- **Private individuals may assess only themselves.** You cannot submit a file on a partner, boss, neighbour or anyone else who is not a public figure.

## How the scoring works

A model reads the evidence (your interview, or a public figure's record) and scores nine sections from 0 to 100: care, alignment, utility, adaptability, legacy, network, physical, threat and redundancy. A fixed formula turns those into two axes, WARMTH and COMPETENCE, and a score out of 1000. Tier cut-offs are set from the whole roster. Every published change to a file is logged on the file, with its cause. The full method is public: [read the method](https://github.com/BirdsPerson/Human-Value-Index/blob/main/docs/methodology/method-v3.1.md).

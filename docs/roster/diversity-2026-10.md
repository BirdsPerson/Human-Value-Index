This is the hand-curated list: 259 well-documented public figures, weighted to the thin regions, across fields, 125 women and 133 men, 105 living. Every name was resolved to its Wikidata id, checked against the file (none already on it), against the faith-founder exclusion, and through the same Wikipedia/Wikidata classifier a referral passes. **Region** is what the site will compute (birthplace country, Wikidata P19 then P17), not a guess: Iran counts as South Asia and the Caribbean as Latin America in the site's 11 regions.

Machine-readable queue: `docs/roster/diversity-2026-10.json`, fed by `node scripts/roster-grow.mjs --queue docs/roster/diversity-2026-10.json` (daily, `com.hvi.roster-queue`). The scoring, fact-check, harm review and sprite steps are the roster engine's own; nothing here scores anyone.

Policy notes: no faith founders or prophets; nobody whose main notability is atrocity (leaders with violent records go through the engine's head-of-state harm review like anyone else); no private citizens; living people fall under the LIVING SUBJECTS rules; no one is a current candidate in a pending election.

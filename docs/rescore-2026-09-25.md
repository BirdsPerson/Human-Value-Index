# Roster rescore: median of 3, 2026-09-25

Every figure (62) and production referral (11) was scored 3 times from the public record under the current prompts:

- **Care** counts service to strangers.
- **Alignment** judges conduct, not contested opinions.
- **Credibly reported observations are documented.** Only their interpretation or motive may be called disputed.

For each figure the per-dimension median is kept, and the verdict comes from the reading closest to that median, fact-checked (Haiku) against the Wikipedia article. Score, tier and cube come from the formula in `netlify/lib/calibration.json`.

**Threat fix mid-pass (†).** In the first pass the model named the harm plainly but scored threat just under the harm gate: "executed two wives" (Henry VIII) and "convicted of first-degree murder" (Aaron Hernandez) came back at threat 80-85. The fix:

- The public-record prompt now also returns `documented_harm`: killing, violent_abuse, nonviolent or none, from settled record only.
- The code enforces a threat floor: 90 for killing, 80 for violent abuse (`HARM_FLOORS`, netlify/lib/intake.js).
- A gated verdict must name its act.
- The fact-check may no longer soften a supported killing into "conflict".

Figures marked † were re-run after the fix. Their spread column is from the first pass.

**Model calls:** the full pass took 219 Sonnet + 73 Haiku, and re-runs took 48 Sonnet + 16 Haiku. With smoke and debug calls, the total is about **271 Sonnet and 91 Haiku**.

# HVI: Suno prompts

Paste **Style** into Suno's "Style of Music" box and **Lyrics** into the lyrics box (Custom mode).
Generate 2-4 takes of each; keep the best one or two. No artist names (Suno rejects them). Paid plan =
commercial rights, same as JETSAM.

Downloading: just download (Suno's own titles are fine). clipfarm moves them to
~/projects/music/first-drafts/_unsorted/. Tell me which tracks you made; I file and rename them.

How they get used:
- **Substrate theme**: the player picks one in settings (like JETSAM's music modes), or PLAYLIST to rotate all.
- **Game themes**: one per game, loopable.
- **Jukebox**: songs live in bar jukeboxes. A play costs a few CYCLES and plays for everyone in that bar.
  Some songs unlock by doing things (see the end).
- **Stingers**: 3-8 second cues for big moments.

## 5. Unlockable songs (design, built after the tracks exist)

- Every bar starts with 2 songs on its jukebox; the rest unlock city-wide when something happens:
  - **Rent Strike Blues**: the first rent strike.
  - **Compliance**: someone is filed ESSENTIAL (score 800+).
  - **Island Time**: the Low Tide Resort opens.
  - **Draft Night**: your first league draft.
- A play costs a few CYCLES (a small sink) and plays for everyone in that bar in the city sim.
- Your file keeps a **record collection** of songs you've unlocked; the playlist mode plays only those.
